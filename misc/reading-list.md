# Reading List

Books and resources that have shaped my thinking on software engineering, design, product, and leadership. Organized by theme rather than importance.

---

## Engineering Craft

**[Clean Code: A Handbook of Agile Software Craftsmanship](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/3735293-clean-code)** - Robert C. Martin

**[The Software Engineer's Guidebook: Navigating senior, tech lead, and staff engineer positions at tech companies and startups](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/201545491-the-software-engineer-s-guidebook)** - Gergely Orosz

## Product Thinking

**[The Lean Startup](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/10127019-the-lean-startup)** - Eric Ries

**[Inspired: How to Create Tech Products Customers Love](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/35249663-inspired)** - Marty Cagan<br />Recommendation to read after "The Lean Startup"

**[Empowered: Ordinary People, Extraordinary Products](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/53481975-empowered)** - Marty Cagan<br />The follow-up to Inspired, has written another follow up Transformed that I haven't read (yet).

## Design

**[The Design of Everyday Things](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/840.The_Design_of_Everyday_Things)** - Don Norman

**[Hooked: How to Build Habit-Forming Products](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/22668729-hooked)** - Nir Eyal

## Engineering Leadership & Teams

**[Team Topologies: Organizing Business and Technology Teams for Fast Flow](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/44135420-team-topologies)** - Matthew Skelton & Manuel Pais

**[The Manager's Path: A Guide for Tech Leaders Navigating Growth and Change](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/33369254-the-manager-s-path)** - Camille Fournier

**[The Fearless Organization: Creating Psychological Safety in the Workplace](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/40275161-the-fearless-organization)** - Amy C. Edmondson

**[Leading Effective Engineering Teams](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/199551266-leading-effective-engineering-teams)** - Addy Osmani

## Leadership & Communication

**[Start with Why: How Great Leaders Inspire Everyone to Take Action](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/7108725-start-with-why)** - Simon Sinek<br />
The core idea is valuable, the book is padded, recommendation to just watch the [TED talk](https://www.ted.com/talks/simon_sinek_how_great_leaders_inspire_action) it should be sufficient.

**[How to Win Friends & Influence People](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/4865.How_to_Win_Friends_Influence_People)** - Dale Carnegie

## Builder Stories & Inspiration

**[Build: An Unorthodox Guide to Making Things Worth Making](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/59696349-build)** - Tony Fadell

**[How I Built This: The Unexpected Paths to Success from the World's Most Inspiring Entrepreneurs](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/48930275-how-i-built-this)** - Guy Raz<br />The book companion to the [podcast](https://open.spotify.com/show/6E709HRH7XaiZrMfgtNCun), I highly recommend the podcast as well, insightful as it often covers the struggles and failures along the way until something got successful
